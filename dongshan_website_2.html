About Our Company

Committed to Delivering Innovative Construction Solutions

We are a Construction company committed to delivering innovative solutions and exceptional services to our valued clients. Our expertise spans Civil Construction, Interior, Clean Room, and MEP. We strive for excellence in every aspect of our operations — giving architectural interior maximum practicality through the value of volume.

Originally established in Shanghai, China, Dong Shan Construction was formally registered in India in 2019 through Samsung's support, and has since served global corporations including Samsung, Hyundai, KIA, and KB Kookmin Bank.

What We Do

Our Core Services

Complete infrastructural solutions — from civil foundations to interior finishes, clean rooms to MEP systems.

01 — Civil

Civil Construction

Full civil works including brick work, plaster, structural gypsum framing, epoxy flooring, self-levelling, waterproofing, and complete site execution.

  • Gypsum structure & framing
  • Epoxy & conductive flooring
  • Self-levelling & waterproofing
  • Brick work & plaster
02 — Interior

Interior Fit-Out

Premium interior solutions — gypsum boards (normal, fire-rated, CRC), false ceilings, LVT/tile flooring, glass partitions, joinery, putty, sanding, and paint.

  • Normal, Fire-Rated & CRC boards
  • Grid & gypsum ceilings
  • Floor, wall & LVT tile
  • Putty, sanding & texture paint
03 — Modular

Modular Wall & Systems

Modular wall systems, toilet cubicles (Merino/Greenlam), rolling/auto shutters, glass doors, fire-rated doors, curtain boxes, and panel wall installations.

  • Glass doors & partitions
  • Fire-rated & double doors
  • Rolling & auto shutters
  • Toilet cubicles & dock levellers
04 — Clean Room

Clean Room Works

Specialised clean room construction for high-precision environments — aluminium profile, toughened glass, epoxy walls and ceilings, and conductive tile flooring.

  • Clean room wall & ceiling epoxy
  • Aluminium profile & glass work
  • Conductive tile flooring
  • Stair protectors & non-slip tape
05 — MEP

MEP Services

Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing services including HVAC, electrical work, and complete MEP integration for industrial and commercial projects.

  • HVAC systems
  • Electrical installation & wiring
  • Plumbing & sanitary
  • AC & ventilation integration
06 — HSE

HSE & Safety Compliance

Rigorous Health, Safety & Environment standards on every site. Safety assessment, PPE compliance, and certified Safety First culture — Samsung Safety Champion 2020.

  • Safety assessment & profiling
  • PPE compliance management
  • Environmental policy adherence
  • Quality policy enforcement
